Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join the competitive varsity team? +
You must be an active Competitive Tier member (RM15/semester). Tryouts are held at the beginning of each semester for major titles like CS2, Valorant, and League of Legends.
What is the difference between Casual and Competitive tier membership? +
Casual Tier (RM5/semester) grants access to our social Discord channels, weekly in-house scrim nights, and viewing parties. Competitive Tier (RM15/semester) includes varsity tryout eligibility, sponsored tournament entry fees, jersey discounts, and priority coaching sessions.
Can non-UTAR students join events? +
Social watch parties and open LAN scrims are open to the public! However, inter-varsity tournaments and main roster positions are restricted to current UTAR students.
Where do weekly club scrims take place? +
Most casual scrims take place online via our official Discord server on Friday and Sunday evenings at 8:00 PM MYT. Physical LAN scrims are held at Pavilion Hall during mid-semester breaks.
How do I register a team for upcoming tournaments? +
Team captains can submit a registration request through the Tournaments page or use the contact form above with the "Tournament Registration" topic, including all 5 players' student IDs and in-game names.
Does the club provide gaming equipment for LAN tournaments? +
For on-campus LAN tournaments, high-refresh-rate monitors and network switches are provided. Participants are encouraged to bring their own personal peripherals (mouse, keyboard, headset, and mousepad).
