WELCOME TO UTAR ESPORT

Empowering student gamers at UTAR to connect, compete, and excel in regional and national esports tournaments.

WHO WE ARE

MORE THAN JUST A GAMING CLUB

UTAR Esport Club is the university's home for competitive gaming. We bring together students from every faculty — players, shoutcasters, analysts and organisers — and give them a place to train, compete and represent UTAR on the regional and national stage.

WHAT WE DO

THREE WAYS TO GET INVOLVED

Compete

We field teams in Valorant, Mobile Legends, League of Legends and more, from campus scrims right up to national inter-university championships.

Train

Weekly practice nights, VOD reviews and coaching sessions run by our senior players, so newcomers improve fast and veterans stay sharp.

Connect

LAN parties, watch parties and community tournaments open to all members — the easiest way to meet people who play what you play.
