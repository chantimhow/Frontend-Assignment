ABOUT US

Made for students, by students — This is the story of UTAR's home for competitive gaming.

WHO WE ARE

UTAR Esport Club is the official home for student players at Universiti Tunku Abdul Rahman. We bring together gamers across every level from casual players to national-level competitors.

We organise the in-house lobbies, inter-faculty wars, and inter-varsity championships, while providing coaching, VOD reviews, and a community where every rank is welcome.
